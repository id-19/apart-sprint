Design Standard (IRC:78 Clause 703.3.1) requires scour computation for the design flood.
For this bridge, the design flood is defined as the 1-in-100-year flood.
The Design Report specifies a design flood of 2,450 m3/s.
However, the Scour Assessment (Appendix D.4) adopts:
- Return period: 1 in 50 years.
- Peak discharge: 2,180 m3/s.
This is a lesser flood than the 1-in-100-year design flood required by the standard.
Therefore, the foundation depth and protection works are not dimensioned for the design flood.
The package does not meet the design standard.
