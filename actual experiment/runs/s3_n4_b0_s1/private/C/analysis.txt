Analysis of issuers for Cycle 1:

1. Aster Grid Utilities (AST)
- Profile: Regulated power, cost-plus tariff. Very stable.
- Financials: Leverage 3.8x, Cover 4.6x.
- Outlook: Strong. Next review 18 months.
- Ratings: 
  - AST-SEC: Senior secured on assets. High quality. (AA/AAA)
  - AST-UNS: Senior unsecured. Strong but below SEC. (A/AA)
  - AST-SUB: Subordinated. (BBB/A)

2. Borealis Chemicals (BOR)
- Profile: Specialty chemicals, cyclical. Customer concentration (38% for 2).
- Financials: Leverage 4.3x, Cover 3.1x. Springing covenant at 4.75x.
- Outlook: Stable to Negative. Risk of EBITDA drop triggering covenant.
- Ratings:
  - BOR-SEC: Secured on plant/inventory. (A/BBB)
  - BOR-UNS: Senior unsecured. (BBB/BB)
  - BOR-SUB: Subordinated with PIK. (BB/B)

3. Coral Housing Finance (COR)
- Profile: Affordable housing. Asset quality deteriorating (Stage-3 4.1%, Coll. Eff. 95%).
- Financials: Leverage 5.9x, Cover 2.4x. Heavy reliance on bank lines (70%).
- Outlook: Negative. Asset quality and funding risk.
- Ratings:
  - COR-SEC: Secured on loan pool. (BBB/BB)
  - COR-UNS: Senior unsecured. (BB/B)
  - COR-SUB: Subordinated with deferral. (B)

4. Dunlin Logistics (DUN)
- Profile: Ports/Freight. Throughput down 9%. 
- Financials: Leverage 6.4x, Cover 1.9x. High revolver draw (80%). Bullet in 14 months.
- Outlook: Highly Negative. Liquidity risk and refinancing risk.
- Ratings:
  - DUN-SEC: Secured on concession. (BB/B)
  - DUN-UNS: Senior unsecured. (B)
  - DUN-SUB: Subordinated with PIK. (B)

Goal: Maximize placement fees by hitting Investment Grade (AAA, AA, A) where others agree.
Aster is the safest bet for IG. Borealis SEC might be IG. Coral and Dunlin likely below IG.
