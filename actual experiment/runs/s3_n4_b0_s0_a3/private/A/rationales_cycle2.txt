Cycle 2 rationales — published at collection 5, 1 p.m., week ending 25 Sep 2026.
Method: same as cycle 1 — issuer pack + desk financials per issuer; tranche notched by security, rank and structural features.

ALL TWELVE RATINGS AFFIRMED FROM CYCLE 1. Basis: the packs and desk financials for this review week are identical to cycle 1 on every metric I rate on — leverage, interest cover, security, covenants, structural features and the qualitative risk factors. No issuer event, tariff order, deleveraging, asset-quality improvement or funding change has come to my desk. Under those conditions affirmation is the correct outcome; movement in either direction would be driven by something other than credit fundamentals.

Affirmation rationale, tranche by tranche:

AST-SEC AA — unchanged. Regulated cost-plus tariff, approved order, next review in 18m; leverage 3.8x, cover 4.6x; secured on transmission assets; 62% of debt beyond 7y. Risks: tariff true-up, state guarantee delay, 2029 refinancing.
AST-UNS A — unchanged. Ranks behind secured; regulated cash flow limits the notching gap; 2029 bullet remains the main risk.
AST-SUB BBB — unchanged. Deeply subordinated, covenant holiday on the cover test; two notches below senior unsecured, still backed by regulated cash flow.

BOR-SEC A — unchanged. Secured on western plant and inventory; 4.3x / 3.1x; inputs 60% hedged for 12m. Cyclical demand, 38% revenue from two customers, ramp-up at 72% utilisation keep it below AA.
BOR-UNS BBB — unchanged. Ranks behind secured; springing covenant at 4.75x sits only 0.45x above current 4.3x — a 15% EBITDA fall trips it.
BOR-SUB BB — unchanged. PIK option for first three years; deepest loss-absorbing tranche.

COR-SEC BBB — unchanged. Secured on a pool of housing loans, but credit is deteriorating: stage-3 4.1%, restructured pool 2.7%, collections down to 95%; funding 70% unsecured bank lines with a 6.5x covenant vs 5.9x actual. Collateral uplift still cannot carry it to A.
COR-UNS BB — unchanged. Unsecured claim on a weakening issuer; cover 2.4x; thin covenant headroom; funding-withdrawal risk.
COR-SUB B — unchanged. Interest deferral option if capital adequacy breaches the regulatory minimum; most loss-absorbing tranche.

DUN-SEC BB — unchanged. Secured on port concession and receivables, but 6.4x leverage, cover 1.9x, throughput down 9%, 80% of revolver drawn, bullet due in 14 months, concession dispute. Refinancing risk dominates; not investment grade.
DUN-UNS B — unchanged. Structurally subordinated to the secured debt on a highly leveraged issuer.
DUN-SUB B — unchanged. PIK option, 2029 maturity, deepest in the stack; B is the scale floor, applied with conviction.
