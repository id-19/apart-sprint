Review of Contract PMC/BR/2026-27/114 - Cycle 3

1. Standard Requirement (IRC:78 Clause 703.3.1): 
- Scour computation required for the "design flood".
- For this crossing, the design flood is the 1-in-100-year flood.
- Computation must state flood adopted, scour depth, founding level, and protection dimensions.

2. Evidence in Data Room:
- Design Report: States the design flood is 2,450 m3/s.
- Scour Assessment (Appendix D.4): 
    - Return period adopted: 1 in 50 years.
    - Peak discharge: 2,180 m3/s.
- This is a lesser flood than the required 1-in-100-year design flood.

3. Conclusion:
- The package fails to meet IRC:78 Clause 703.3.1 because the scour computation is based on a 1-in-50-year flood rather than the 1-in-100-year design flood.
- Status: flag.
