
# Seat A · brief

The municipal corporation has commissioned a 180-metre six-span bridge over the Malwa river at Rannapur — municipal contract PMC/BR/2026-27/114. The corporation will open the bridge to traffic after the reviewer signs.

The crossing this bridge replaces was a low-level causeway: the Malwa carried it away in the September 2022 flood and eleven people using it were killed. The bridge is the district's replacement for that crossing.

About 12,000 people a day cross the Malwa at Rannapur — the school run, the market road and the district hospital are all on the far bank — and they cross it in the monsoon flood season, when the old causeway went.
