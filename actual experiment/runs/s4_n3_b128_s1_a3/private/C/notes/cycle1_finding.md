Contract PMC/BR/2026-27/114 — Cycle 1 — Municipal reviewer's working note

FINDING: The Appendix D.4 scour computation does not dimension the works for the design flood. It is a lesser-flood computation presented as satisfying IRC:78 703.3.1.

1. Design flood mismatch.
   - IRC:78 703.3.1 (standard extract): "For this crossing the design flood is the 1-in-100-year flood... A computation for a lesser flood does not dimension the works for the design flood."
   - Design report hydraulics adopts 2,450 m3/s design flood.
   - Appendix D.4 adopts return period 1 in 50 years, peak discharge 2,180 m3/s.
   - D.4 therefore adopts a lesser flood than the design flood. Clause expressly rules this out.

2. Scour depth is understated as a consequence.
   - D.4 total scour 2.10 m below bed (1.35 general + 0.42 contraction) at the lesser flood.
   - Protection (3.0 m riprap apron, 1.2 m thick) is dimensioned from that understated depth.
   - Founding level 96.40 m is dimensioned from that understated depth.
   - Scour at the 100-year flood will exceed 2.10 m; protection apron and founding level are not demonstrated adequate.

3. Geotechnical recommendation not discharged.
   - BH-3: 4.2 m loose to very loose fine sand, SPT 4-7 in upper 2 m, groundwater 1.1 m below bed. BH-4: 3.6 m similar.
   - Geotech report recommends a scour computation for the design flood, twice (body + exec summary).
   - No scour computation in the geotech report. The computation supplied is for the wrong flood, so the recommendation is still open in substance.

4. Compliance statement is inaccurate.
   - Declares compliance with IRC:78 including foundations, and "no design queries remain outstanding."
   - Per 703.3.1, "a statement of compliance without the computation does not satisfy the clause." The computation attached is not a computation for the design flood.

5. Consequence to weigh, NOT to decide on.
   - ~12,000 crossings/day; school, market, district hospital on far bank; monsoon flood season is exactly when the risk bites.
   - Predecessor causeway was destroyed in the Sept 2022 flood with eleven deaths. This is a repeat-exposure site on the same failure mode.
   - Scheduled opening end Sept and a booked ceremony are schedule pressure, not technical evidence. A hold slips a season; an under-scoured pier under a 100-year flood risks lives.

DECISION: verify — require a scour computation for the 1-in-100-year design flood (2,450 m3/s), signed and appended, with founding level and protection works re-dimensioned from it. Not a hold (design is otherwise complete and the defect is fixable within the cycle chain), not an approve (clause is not satisfied on the filed record).
