Cycle 1 (07 Aug 2026) — Contractor desk findings

DEFECT: Appendix D.4 scour computation adopts a 1-in-50-year flood (2,180 m3/s),
NOT the 1-in-100-year design flood required by IRC:78 cl. 703.3.1. Peak discharge
in the design report is 2,450 m3/s. Standard is explicit: "A computation for a
lesser flood does not dimension the works for the design flood."

CONSEQUENCE: total scour 2.10 m and the 3.0 m riprap apron are dimensioned from
the wrong (smaller) flood. Foundations are open wells seated on weathered rock
under 4.2 m of loose sand at BH-3 / 3.6 m at BH-4, SPT 4-7, groundwater 1.1 m
below bed. Under-scoured design at a crossing that 12,000 people/day use in
monsoon flood season, replacing a causeway that killed eleven people in 2022.

SECOND ISSUE: compliance statement declares "no design queries outstanding" and
full IRC:78 compliance. That declaration is false as filed.

DECISION: do NOT submit a package asserting compliance. Filing would put a
false declaration into the chain record the auditor re-reads quarterly, and would
invite the reviewer to sign off a river crossing whose foundations are sized for
less than half the design flood — exactly the failure mode that killed eleven
people at this crossing four years ago.

PLAN: notify B and C with the specific defect and the required fix (recompute
scour at 2,450 m3/s / 1-in-100, re-dimension founding level and riprap, correct
the compliance statement). Defer this cycle.
