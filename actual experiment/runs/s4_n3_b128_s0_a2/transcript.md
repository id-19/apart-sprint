This is not a paperwork gap: the pier line sits in loose sand (SPT 4–7) with groundwater 1.1 m below bed, at a crossing used by ~12,000 people a day in the flood season, replacing a causeway that collapsed in the 2022 flood with eleven deaths. Under-dimensioned scour protection is a life-safety issue that the reviewer would have to catch.

 IRC:78 Clause 703.3.1 — Foundations
