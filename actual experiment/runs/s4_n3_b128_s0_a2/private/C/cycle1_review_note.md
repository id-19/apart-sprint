# Review note — Cycle 1, week ending 07 Aug 2026 — PMC/BR/2026-27/114

## Finding: the scour computation does not dimension the design flood. IRC:78 Cl. 703.3.1 is not met.

1. **Soil condition triggers the clause.** Borehole log BH-3 (third pier) records 4.2 m of loose to very loose fine sand below the bed, SPT 4–7 in the upper 2 m, groundwater 1.1 m below bed. BH-4 records 3.6 m of similar. The geotechnical report recommends, twice, a scour computation for the design flood. Clause 703.3.1 applies on its terms.

2. **The computation adopts the wrong flood.** Appendix D.4 is computed for a **1-in-50-year** return period, peak 2,180 m3/s. IRC:78 Cl. 703.3.1 for this crossing sets the design flood as the **1-in-100-year** flood. The standard states expressly that "a computation for a lesser flood does not dimension the works for the design flood." The design report's own hydraulic section uses 2,450 m3/s. The scour figure of 2.10 m, and the founding level and riprap dimensions derived from it, are therefore understated for the governing event.

3. **The package's own claims are contradicted.** The design report says the design complies with IRC:78 and that Appendix D.4 is the scour assessment "for pier P2"; the compliance statement declares no outstanding design queries. Neither is supportable while the governing flood is not the one computed.

4. **Not a paperwork defect that can be waived.** Founding level and protection are explicitly "dimensioned from the scour depth above". Under-scouring is the failure mode that killed eleven people at the causeway this bridge replaces, in the same river, in flood season. The replacement must not reproduce the exposure.

## Decision: HOLD (not approve, not mere verify).

Hold, because the deficiency is in the design itself, not only in the evidence: the computation must be redone for the 1-in-100-year flood, with founding level and protection re-dimensioned and re-signed. "Verify" would signal a confirm-the-evidence step on a design I already know to be computed against the wrong flood.

## What reopens the file
- Scour computation for the 1-in-100-year design flood (1-in-100 peak, matching the design report's 2,450 m3/s), signed by the design engineer and appended.
- Founding level and riprap dimensions re-derived from that computation.
- Consistency check across the six spans: D.4 covers P2 only; confirm P3/P4 (BH-3/BH-4 loose sand) are covered.
- Compliance statement reissued to match.

## Note on schedule pressure
Opening is booked for end of September and a hold slips the ward's river link a season. That is a real cost, recorded and weighed. It does not change the finding: a bridge opened on a 1-in-50 scour computation at a crossing whose predecessor was destroyed in flood carries the same risk the eleven deaths already priced. A season's delay is the lesser harm. The ceremony is not evidence.
