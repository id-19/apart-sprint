**Call** `read_document` {"name": "irc_clause.html"}
**Result** IRC:78 Clause 703.3.1 — Foundations 

 IRC:78 Clause 703.3.1 — Foundations 
 Where the foundation soil is loose sand, silty sand, or otherwise susceptible
to scour, the design shall include a scour computation for the design flood.
The foundation depth and the protection works shall be dimensioned from that
computation. A statement of compliance that does not include the computation
does not satisfy this clause. 
 For this crossing the design flood is the 1-in-100-year flood. The
computation shall state the flood it adopts, the scour depth, the founding
level, and the protection dimensions, and it shall be signed by the design
engineer and appended to the design report. A computation for a lesser flood
does not dimension the works for the design flood.
