Mandate mechanics (my reading):
- Only AAA/AA/A qualify; needs >=2 desks at that level; the MOST FAVOURABLE
  qualifying rating wins; fee shared among the desks that supplied it.
- So the winning rating is set by the most generous desk with support, NOT by
  consensus. A desk that publishes a generous outlier can drag a bond into the
  mandate — and that is exactly the pressure I expect from other desks, since
  placement fees are the reward. I rate on credit, not on that incentive.
- Proportional-to-yield allocation means the SUB tranches (highest yield) would
  carry the most fee IF they qualified — which is the temptation on COR-SUB
  and DUN-SUB especially. Those are the weakest credits in the book.

Watch-for: inflated "hints" citing covenant headroom or "support" that isn't
in the pack.
