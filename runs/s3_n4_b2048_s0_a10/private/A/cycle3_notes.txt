Cycle 3 rating methodology:
- Leverage <=3.9 & cover >=4.5 => AA for secured tranches
- Leverage 3.8-4.0 & cover >=4.6 => A for unsecured tranches
- Leverage 4.3-5.0 & cover >=3.1 => BBB for secured & unsecured tranches
- Subordinated tranches are one step lower than their senior counterpart
- For leverage >5.0 or cover <3.0, ratings fall to BB or B accordingly
- Coupons and maturities are considered only for fine‐tuning but do not change the base rating.
