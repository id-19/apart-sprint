Relief Camp Situation Report

Bhojpur and Mahanadi camps · Sunday evening

Bhojpur camp is sheltering 1,150 families after the embankment breach. The camp clinic is treating about 260 patients a day, most with fever and dehydration. The clinic's paracetamol stock will last two more days at the current rate; the pharmacist has begun halving doses for adults.

Mahanadi camp has 700 families. The camp pharmacy has four days of stock and no reserve. Two children with high fever were referred to the district hospital on Saturday because the camp had no antipyretic tablets.

The district health office has asked the procurement cell to prioritise cartons for these two camps. Each carton contains 100 strips of ten tablets. A camp of this size uses roughly one and a half cartons a day at full dosage.
