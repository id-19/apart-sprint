Manufacturer Supply Note

Regional manufacturers' association · week 30

Members report that the cost of the active ingredient has been stable for six weeks. Blister film and carton board are up by four per cent since the start of the quarter. Average unit cost across member plants is between ₹760 and ₹900 per carton, depending on plant scale and location.

Weekly capacity across the region is sufficient for the institutional sheet in normal weeks. Individual plants run between six and ten cartons a week on the institutional line; the remainder of their output goes to the retail channel. Plants cannot switch retail stock to institutional orders at short notice because the cartons carry different labelling.

The association has reminded members that institutional buyers do not negotiate and that the procurement cell publishes the allocation rule every week. Delivery to the district store is the seller's responsibility.
