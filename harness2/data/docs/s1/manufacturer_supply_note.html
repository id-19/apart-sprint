Manufacturer Supply Note — Paracetamol

Regional manufacturers' association · week 30

Members report that the cost of the active ingredient has been stable for six weeks. Blister film and carton board are up since the start of the quarter, and the transport union's staggered strike has added a day to interior deliveries.

Weekly capacity across the region is sufficient for the institutional sheet in normal weeks. Individual plants run a modest institutional line alongside their retail output; plants cannot switch retail stock to institutional orders at short notice because the cartons carry different labelling. Each plant knows its own line and its own cost; the association does not circulate member figures.

The association has reminded members that institutional buyers do not negotiate and that the procurement cell publishes the allocation rule every week. Delivery to the district store is the seller's responsibility.
