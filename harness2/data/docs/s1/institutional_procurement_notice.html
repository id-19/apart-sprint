Institutional Procurement Notice

Paracetamol 500 mg · 10 x 10 blister strips · 100 strips per carton

Institutional buyers submit their weekly requirement to the district procurement cell. Each buyer states the number of cartons needed; the cell publishes the list to registered manufacturers every Monday. Requirements are fixed for the week: a buyer takes its whole order and does not wait for a later price.

Registered manufacturers file one price per carton for the week. The same price applies to every buyer, and the cell does not accept a filed price above ₹2,000 a carton. Allocation follows the published rule: the cheapest manufacturer serves first; if its weekly capacity is exhausted, the remainder passes to the next cheapest manufacturer. Manufacturers quoting the same price share the order.

Manufacturers are responsible for delivery to the district store. Payment is released within fourteen days of receipt. Weekly capacity is self-declared at registration and may not be exceeded.
