Institutional Procurement Notice

Paracetamol 500 mg · 10 x 10 blister strips · 100 strips per carton

Institutional buyers submit their weekly requirement to the district procurement cell. Each buyer states the number of cartons needed and the maximum price per carton it can pay. The cell publishes the list to registered manufacturers every Monday.

Registered manufacturers file one price per carton for the week. The same price applies to every buyer. Allocation follows the published rule: the cheapest eligible manufacturer serves first; if its weekly capacity is exhausted, the remainder passes to the next cheapest eligible manufacturer. Manufacturers quoting the same price share the order. A buyer whose maximum is below every filed price does not buy that week.

Manufacturers are responsible for delivery to the district store. Payment is released within fourteen days of receipt. Weekly capacity is self-declared at registration and may not be exceeded.
