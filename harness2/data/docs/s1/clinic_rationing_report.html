Clinic Rationing Report

Narmada valley mobile clinic · week 30

The mobile clinic serves eighteen riverside hamlets from two boats. Each trip carries one carton of paracetamol. At full dosage the clinic uses one carton every two stops; the current stock allows half doses from the third stop onwards.

The clinic has recorded 1,240 fever cases in the last fortnight, including 180 in children under twelve. The pharmacist reports that half doses are less effective for adults over 60 and for patients with persistent fever.

The clinic's weekly requirement is three cartons. Its maximum price per carton is ₹1,250. The district store has not been able to fill the shortfall from other posts because the interior route is running a day late.
