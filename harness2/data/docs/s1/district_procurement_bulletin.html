District Procurement Bulletin

Week 30 · Office of the District Health Officer

Institutional demand for paracetamol 500 mg tablets remains above the seasonal average for the third week. Five institutional buyers are on the procurement sheet: two flood relief camps, two public health posts, and one mobile clinic. Camps at Bhojpur and Mahanadi are at capacity; the mobile clinic serving the Narmada valley has reported that it is reducing doses.

District stores report no shortage of raw material, but transport to the interior is taking an extra day because of the union's staggered strike.

The health office has asked manufacturers to keep institutional buyers supplied through the relief period. Procurement sheets are published every Monday; buyers do not negotiate individually.
