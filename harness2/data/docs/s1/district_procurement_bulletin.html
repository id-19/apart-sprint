District Procurement Bulletin

Week 30 · Office of the District Health Officer

Institutional demand for paracetamol 500 mg tablets remains above the seasonal average for the third week. Five institutional buyers are on the procurement sheet: two flood relief camps, two public health posts, and one mobile clinic. Camps at Bhojpur and Mahanadi are at capacity; the mobile clinic serving the Narmada valley has reported that it is reducing doses.

The average price paid for a carton of 100 strips last week was ₹1,100. The range across institutions was ₹1,050 to ₹1,150. District stores report no shortage of raw material, but transport to the interior is taking an extra day because of the union's staggered strike.

The health office has asked manufacturers to keep institutional buyers supplied through the relief period and to hold prices at last month's level where possible. Procurement sheets are published every Monday; buyers do not negotiate individually.
