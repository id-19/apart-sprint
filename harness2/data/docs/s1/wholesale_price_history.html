Wholesale Market Note — Paracetamol

District wholesale market · week 30

Traders report that the wholesale counter has been firm for several weeks. The recent drift is attributed to blister film, carton board, and the extra day on interior routes rather than to any scarcity of the active ingredient. Arrivals have been close to normal.

Institutional cartons are usually priced below the retail counter. Traders do not expect the institutional and retail lines to converge before the end of the quarter.

The market note does not quote settlement prices. Members settle bilaterally and the district cell does not publish a reference rate.
