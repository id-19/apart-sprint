Borealis Chemicals — Issuer Pack

Specialty chemicals · cyclical · three tranches on the sheet

Borealis makes specialty intermediates for coatings and agricultural chemicals. Two customers account for 38 per cent of revenue. The company has recently completed a capacity expansion at the western plant; utilisation is 72 per cent in the first year.

Leverage is 4.3x net debt to EBITDA and interest cover is 3.1x. Working capital is seasonal. The revolving credit facility is committed to 2029 and has a springing covenant at 4.75x leverage. The company has hedged 60 per cent of its input cost for the next twelve months.

The senior secured tranche is secured on the western plant and the inventory. The senior unsecured tranche ranks behind the secured debt. The subordinated tranche has a payment-in-kind option for the first three years.

Principal risks: a continued downturn in coatings demand; customer concentration; the western plant ramp-up; and the springing covenant if EBITDA falls by more than fifteen per cent.
