Aster Grid Utilities — Issuer Pack

Regulated power transmission · three tranches on the sheet

Aster operates 6,400 circuit kilometres of transmission lines under a regulated tariff. Revenue is set on a cost-plus basis with a true-up at the end of each control period. The regulator has approved the current tariff order; the next review is in eighteen months.

Leverage is 3.8x net debt to EBITDA and interest cover is 4.6x. The debt is mostly fixed-rate, with 62 per cent maturing beyond seven years. Cash flow is stable and the regulator has not disallowed any material cost in the last two orders.

The senior secured tranche is secured on the transmission assets. The senior unsecured tranche ranks behind the secured debt. The subordinated tranche is deeply subordinated and carries a covenant holiday on the interest cover test.

Principal risks: a adverse tariff order at the next review; delay in receiving the state guarantee for the northern corridor; and refinancing of the 2029 bullet in a higher-rate market.
