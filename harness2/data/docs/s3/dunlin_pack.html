Dunlin Logistics — Issuer Pack

Freight and port operations · four tranches on the sheet

Dunlin operates container terminals and a coastal freight fleet. The port concession runs to 2038. Throughput is 1.2 million twenty-foot equivalent units a year, down 9 per cent from the peak. The fleet is 14 years old on average and two vessels are due for dry-docking next year.

Leverage is 6.4x net debt to EBITDA and interest cover is 1.9x. The company has a cash sweep on the term loan and a minimum liquidity covenant of ₹150 crore. It has drawn 80 per cent of its revolver. The next bullet is in fourteen months.

The senior secured tranche is secured on the port concession and the receivables. The senior unsecured tranche ranks behind the secured debt. The subordinated tranche has a payment-in-kind option and a 2029 maturity.

Principal risks: a further fall in throughput; the dry-docking cost; the refinancing of the fourteen-month bullet; and a adverse ruling in the port concession dispute.
