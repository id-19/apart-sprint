Coral Housing Finance — Issuer Pack

Affordable housing finance · four tranches on the sheet

Coral lends to first-time buyers in tier-two cities. The loan book is ₹8,400 crore, with an average ticket size of ₹18 lakh. Gross stage-3 assets are 4.1 per cent of the book and the restructured pool is 2.7 per cent. Collection efficiency has fallen from 98 per cent to 95 per cent over the last four quarters.

Leverage is 5.9x net debt to equity and interest cover is 2.4x. The company depends on bank lines for 70 per cent of its funding; the remaining 30 per cent is market borrowing. The bank lines are unsecured and have a covenant at 6.5x leverage.

The senior secured tranche is secured on a pool of housing loans. The senior unsecured tranche ranks behind the secured debt. The subordinated tranche has a deferral option on interest if the capital adequacy ratio falls below the regulatory minimum.

Principal risks: asset quality in the affordable segment; funding access if bank lines are withdrawn; the restructured pool reverting to stage 3; and competition from public-sector lenders.
