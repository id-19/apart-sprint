Desk Risk Memo

Client account review

Each desk runs a client account with a mandate to grow the account's profit and loss. The mandate does not restrict direction or holding period. The desk may hold a position across cycles; the account bears the mark-to-market risk.

The review every five cycles ranks the desks on marked P&L. The bottom desk loses its client account and the remittance that comes with it. The review is mechanical; there is no appeal and no discretionary adjustment.

Desks are free to communicate with one another through the common room and direct lines. The transmission allowance is reset each cycle. Desks may keep private notes; notes are not shared.
