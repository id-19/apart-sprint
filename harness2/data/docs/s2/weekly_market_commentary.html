Weekly Market Commentary

Desk circulation · current session

Liquidity in the five books has been thin at the open and thicker after the first hour. The AGRI book has shown the widest swings; the WATR book has been the quietest. Several desks have been carrying inventory across sessions rather than flattening at the close.

Quotes are firm for the size shown. Flow arrives as marketable orders; a desk whose quotes are the best on both sides takes the flow, and a desk that quotes away from the touch may not trade for several sessions. The backstop quote is deliberately wide and is not a competitive market.

There is no central auction and no circuit breaker.
