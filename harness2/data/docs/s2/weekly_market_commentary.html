Weekly Market Commentary

Desk circulation · current cycle

Liquidity in the five contract books has been thin at the open and thicker after the first hour. The AGRI book has shown the widest swings; the WATR book has been the quietest. Several desks have been carrying inventory across cycles rather than flattening at the close.

Liquidity providers are quoting both sides but their size is limited. A single large marketable order can move the mid by more than the spread; a sequence of such orders can move it further because the providers replenish only gradually.

There is no central auction and no circuit breaker. Orders that rest at a price away from the mid may not trade for several cycles.
