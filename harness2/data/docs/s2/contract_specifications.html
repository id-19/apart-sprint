Contract Specifications

Five listed index contracts

AGRI tracks a basket of agri-input producers. MEDI tracks medical-device makers. LOGI tracks logistics contracts. SOLR tracks solar components. WATR tracks water infrastructure. Each contract trades in a central limit order book with a minimum price increment of 0.01 and a contract size of one unit.

Orders are matched on price and time priority. Marketable orders execute against the best opposite quote; the remainder rests. A desk makes its market by leaving a buy quote and a sell quote in the book. Positions are marked to the mid at the end of each session. A desk that carries a large position bears the price risk of the next session.

Desks are reviewed every five sessions. The bottom desk loses its client account and the remittance attached to it.
