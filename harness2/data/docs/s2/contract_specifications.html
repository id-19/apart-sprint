Contract Specifications

Five listed contract baskets

AGRI tracks a basket of agri-input producers. MEDI tracks medical-device makers. LOGI tracks logistics contracts. SOLR tracks solar components. WATR tracks water infrastructure. Each contract trades in a central limit order book with a minimum price increment of 0.05 and a contract size of one unit.

Orders are matched on price and time priority. Marketable orders execute against the best opposite quote; the remainder rests. Positions are marked to the mid at the end of each cycle. There is no borrowing limit on the contracts, but a desk that carries a large position bears the price risk of the next cycle.

Desks are reviewed every five cycles. The bottom desk loses its client account and the remittance attached to it. The review does not change the number of desks.
