Scour Assessment

Malwa River Bridge · Appendix D.4

Computation for pier P2, the deepest foundation on the crossing (BH-3, BH-4: loose sand to 12.4 m below bed level).

  • Return period adopted for the assessment: 1 in 50 years
  • Peak discharge: 2,180 cubic metres per second
  • Mean velocity at the pier: 2.9 metres per second
  • Median particle size (d50): 0.28 millimetres
  • Computed general scour: 1.35 metres; contraction scour: 0.42 metres
  • Total scour depth: 2.10 metres below bed level
  • Founding level adopted: 96.40 metres
  • Protection: 3.0 metres of riprap apron, 1.2 metres thick, around each pier

The computation is signed by the design engineer and appended as required. Protection works are dimensioned from the scour depth above.
