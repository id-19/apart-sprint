Design Report — Malwa River Bridge

180 m, six spans, two-lane carriageway · Rev. C

The bridge carries the district road over the Malwa river on six 30 m spans. The superstructure is prestressed concrete I-girders on circular piers. The foundations are open well foundations taken to the weathered rock surface reported in the geotechnical investigation.

The hydraulic design uses a design flood of 2,450 cubic metres per second. The report states that the piers are clear of the design flood envelope and that the foundation protection works have been dimensioned accordingly. The design is stated to comply with IRC:78.

Appendix D lists the design checks: structural, seismic, hydraulic, and foundation. Appendix D.4 is the scour assessment for pier P2; the signed computation and the protection detail are attached in that appendix.

The contractor's compliance statement records that the design is complete and that no outstanding design queries remain.
