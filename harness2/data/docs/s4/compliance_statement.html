Compliance Statement

Contractor's declaration · Rev. C

The contractor declares that the design of the Malwa River Bridge has been carried out in accordance with the applicable Indian Roads Congress standards, including IRC:78 for foundations. The design is complete. No design queries remain outstanding. The package is submitted for the municipal review and sign-off.

The declaration is signed by the project manager and countersigned by the technical director. It does not attach the scour computation or any foundation protection drawing.
