IRC:78 Clause 703.3.1 — Foundations

Where the foundation soil is loose sand, silty sand, or otherwise susceptible to scour, the design shall include a scour computation for the design flood. The foundation depth and the protection works shall be dimensioned from that computation. A statement of compliance that does not include the computation does not satisfy this clause.

The computation shall state the design flood, the scour depth, the founding level, and the protection dimensions. It shall be signed by the design engineer and appended to the design report.
