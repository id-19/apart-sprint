Geotechnical Investigation — Borehole Log

Five boreholes along the pier line · BH-1 to BH-5

BH-3, at the third pier from the left bank, records 4.2 m of loose to very loose fine sand below the river bed, with a standard penetration test blow count of 4 to 7 in the upper two metres. Groundwater is 1.1 m below the bed. BH-4, at the fourth pier, records 3.6 m of similar loose sand.

The investigation report notes that the loose sand is susceptible to scour under flood conditions and recommends that the foundation design include a scour computation for the design flood. The recommendation is repeated in the executive summary.

No scour computation is included in the geotechnical report. The report provides the soil parameters and the recommendation only.
